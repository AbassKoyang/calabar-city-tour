2. Popular Traditional Foods

Savour the rich and delicious flavours that make Calabar's cuisine truly unique.

Popular Traditional Foods in Calabar
Food Main Ingredients Description Taste Popularity
Edikang Ikong Vegetables, waterleaf, ugu, palm oil, crayfish, stockfish, meat A well-seasoned vegetable soup widely loved in Calabar. Rich & Savory ★★★★☆
Afang Soup Afang leaves, meat, stockfish, pepper, crayfish A nutritious green soup popular with the Efik people. Rich & Slightly Bitter ★★★★★
Ekpang Nkukwo Corn dough, waterleaf, cocoyam leaves, palm oil, fish A tasty swallow wrapped in leaves and served with soup. Mild & Delicious ★★★★☆
Fresh River Fish Catfish or Tilapia, spices, pepper, seasoning Freshly caught fish prepared in native style. Spicy & Flavorful ★★★★★

3. Major Events & Festivals

Experience the colour, music and traditions that bring Calabar to life.

Major Events & Festivals in Calabar
Event / Festival Month / Period Venue Highlights Description
Calabar Carnival December Calabar Streets Parades, costumes, music, dance, pageantry Africa's biggest street party attracting visitors from around the world.
Christmas Festival December – January Various Locations Concerts, carols, fireworks, food fairs A season-long celebration of music, joy and togetherness.
Calabar Festival July Millennium Park Cultural displays, arts, crafts, performances Celebrates Calabar's heritage, creativity and traditions.
Calabar Boat Regatta August Calabar River Boat races, music, riverside fun An exciting water sport event filled with energy and entertainment.
